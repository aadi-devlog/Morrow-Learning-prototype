# Morrow Learning — Research and design rationale (founder-review revision)

## Product concept

Morrow is a proposed working name for an AI-enabled education and content initiative. The original assignment brief describes an education/content ecosystem but does not supply an official brand name or operational programme details. This prototype therefore uses Morrow as a working identity and clearly marks it as awaiting founder approval.

## Intended audience

The page addresses students, recent graduates and young professionals. The three audience groups are made explicit in the page rather than being left implicit in the headline.

## Sample learning paths

1. **Applied AI Foundations:** AI essentials and limitations, prompting, research/document workflows, fact-checking and responsible use. Illustrative outcome: a sourced research brief and AI-assisted presentation outline.
2. **AI for Content & Workflows:** audience and content briefs, prompt templates, editing, content calendars, repurposing and workflow quality checks. Illustrative outcome: a short content campaign with a reusable planning workflow.
3. **Web & Digital Product Basics:** user needs, page structure, wireframes, HTML/CSS fundamentals and testing. Illustrative outcome: a responsive landing-page prototype with a short design rationale.

These are concept curricula, not confirmed offerings. The live organisation must confirm availability, faculty, duration, cohort format, fees and learning/completion terms.

## Project showcase

All three project previews are original inline SVG mockups and have been matched to the card descriptions:

- Research brief visual: document structure, findings, source notes and comparison chart.
- Content workflow visual: draft, review and ready stages for a weekly content plan.
- Landing-page visual: browser mockup with navigation, hero, CTA and feature cards.

The visuals are explicitly labelled illustrative and are not presented as testimonials, student outcomes or client work.

## Trust and conversion design

The page asks the organisation to publish the information learners need before committing: syllabus, dates, duration, weekly commitment, delivery format, instructor credentials, learner support, total fees, payment/refund terms and completion/certificate criteria. This avoids unsupported claims while making the information architecture commercially useful.

The enquiry form remains a front-end demonstration. Programme-specific buttons preselect the relevant path. CTA clicks emit privacy-safe custom events, and successful demo submission emits the selected programme only. No personal form fields are transmitted or stored. Production launch requires a secure endpoint, admissions/CRM routing, a privacy notice and confirmation email.

## Visual direction

The existing navy, white and blue design system is retained. Real editorial photography remains in the hero and programme cards, while the project gallery uses bespoke vector mockups rather than generic or mismatched stock imagery. This creates clearer correspondence between each advertised skill and its sample output.

## Items requiring founder approval

- Official organisation/brand name and legal identity.
- Confirmed programme titles and syllabus.
- Instructor names, biographies and learner-support arrangements.
- Cohort dates, duration, weekly commitment and delivery format.
- Fees, payment/refund terms and completion/certificate criteria.
- Approved contact details, privacy notice and CRM/admissions endpoint.
